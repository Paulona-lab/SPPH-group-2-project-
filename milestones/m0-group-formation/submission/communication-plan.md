## Primary communication channel

The group will use Microsoft Teams as the primary communication channel for project updates, scheduling, and problem-solving. We will also keep GitHub issues and pull requests updated so the repository reflects the current project status.

## Response expectations

- Check the team channel at least once weekly.
- Respond to direct questions within 24 hours during the work week.
- Post a short status update when blocked or when a task is completed.
- Use the repository and issues to record decisions that affect group work.

## Weekly check-in plan

- Weekly team meeting: every Sunday at 7:00 PM.
- Meeting agenda: progress review, blockers, next steps, and assignment of tasks.


## Decision-making and workflow

- Major decisions will be made during team meetings or in the shared communication channel.
- Smaller tasks can be completed asynchronously, but they should be documented in GitHub issues or commit messages.
- Any change affecting analysis, documentation, or repository structure should be reviewed by at least one other team member before merge.
- When a milestone is approaching, the group will coordinate final review and submission tasks in the team channel.
